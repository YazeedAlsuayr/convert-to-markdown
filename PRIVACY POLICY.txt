Convert to Markdown - Privacy Policy

Convert to Markdown converts files to Markdown entirely on your device.

- Your files are never uploaded. They are read on your PC, and the .md file is saved next to the original.
- The app collects no personal information, no usage statistics and no crash reports.
- Internet use: only when you choose an AI model, the app downloads the model files from Hugging Face
  (huggingface.co). Linked pictures inside Office files are downloaded only when "Describe layout and
  pictures" is turned on. Nothing about you or your files is sent.
- Settings and AI models are stored in the app's own folder on your PC and are deleted when you uninstall it.
