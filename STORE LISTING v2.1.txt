==================== DESCRIPTION ====================

Convert to Markdown turns your documents into clean, editable Markdown text, right on your PC.

Right-click a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel file, a scan or a picture, choose Convert to Markdown, and a .md file appears next to it. Markdown is plain text that keeps headings, lists, tables and links, so it opens in any note app or text editor and works well with AI tools, wikis and search.

Private by design
Everything runs on your computer. Your files are never uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

What it reads
- Typed text in the right reading order: headings, columns, lists, tables, footnotes and links
- Highlights and pen notes you added to a PDF
- Text inside pictures, screenshots, scans, charts and forms, including ticked or circled answers
- English and Arabic, including right-to-left text
- Headers and footers that repeat on every page are written once, and blank pages are marked

Built-in offline AI (optional)
Choose an AI model on first start. It is downloaded once and then works without the internet, on your graphics card. It reads the text in pictures and handwriting and can describe each page's layout. You can also skip the AI: Windows then reads pictures and handwriting itself.

Made for
Students and teachers, office and research work, and anyone who wants the content of their files as text they can search, edit, summarize or reuse.

Supported files: PDF, DOCX, DOCM, PPTX, PPTM, XLSX, XLSM, PNG, JPG, BMP, TIF, GIF.

Free and open source (MIT License).
