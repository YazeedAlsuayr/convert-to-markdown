PUBLISHING CONVERT TO MARKDOWN ON THE MICROSOFT STORE
=====================================================

A. TRY THE STORE VERSION ON YOUR PC FIRST (optional, recommended)
  1. Settings > Apps > Convert to Markdown > Uninstall (the Setup version). Choose "No" when it asks to
     delete the AI: the Store version copies your downloaded model instead of downloading it again.
  2. Double-click "Test Store package.cmd". It builds the Store package and installs a test copy.
  3. Start menu > Convert to Markdown: the welcome screens appear (choose the AI).
  4. Right-click a PDF: "Convert to Markdown" should be at the top of the menu, and in Open with.
  5. When happy: Settings > Apps > Convert to Markdown > Uninstall (removes the test copy).

B. MAKE YOUR DEVELOPER ACCOUNT (once, free for individuals)
  1. Go to https://storedeveloper.microsoft.com and choose "Sign up" as an Individual developer.
  2. Sign in with your Microsoft account (yazeed.aluayr@hotmail.com), then verify yourself with a government ID and a selfie.
  3. Publisher display name: the name people will see as the publisher (for example "Yazeed ...").
     Choose carefully: it is shown on the Store page and on the app.

C. RESERVE THE APP NAME
  1. Partner Center (https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard, signed in with yazeed.aluayr@hotmail.com) > Apps and games > New product > MSIX or PWA app.
  2. Name: Convert to Markdown (or another name if it is taken) > Reserve product name.

D. PUT YOUR IDENTITY INTO THE BUILD
  1. In Partner Center: your app > Product management > Product identity.
  2. Open StoreIdentity.txt (in the project folder) and paste:
       Name=           the value of Package/Identity/Name
       Publisher=      the value of Package/Identity/Publisher   (starts with CN=)
       PublisherName=  the value of Package/Properties/PublisherDisplayName
  3. Save. Double-click "Build Store package.cmd".
     Result: StorePackage\ConvertToMarkdown_2.0.0.0_x64.msix  (this is what you upload)

E. SUBMIT
  Partner Center > your app > Start your submission:
  1. Pricing and availability: Free, all markets (or the ones you want).
  2. Properties: Category "Productivity".
     Privacy policy URL: https://yazeedalsuayr.github.io/convert-to-markdown/
     (put it online first - see G below).
     Support contact: yazeed.alsueer@gmail.com
  3. Age ratings: fill the questionnaire (no violence, no user chat, etc.).
  4. Packages: drag in the .msix from step D.
  5. Store listings > English: description, at least one screenshot (1366x768 or larger),
     for example the welcome screen and a converted .md file.
  6. Submission options > "Why does your app need the runFullTrust capability?":
       "Convert to Markdown is a desktop (WPF) app. It adds an entry to the File Explorer
        right-click menu and runs a bundled local AI engine (llama.cpp) to read text in pictures.
        AI model files are downloaded from Hugging Face only when the user chooses one; all
        processing is on the device and no files are uploaded."
  7. Submit to the Store. Certification usually takes 1-3 working days. You get an email.

F. UPDATES LATER
  1. Raise Version in StoreIdentity.txt (2.0.1.0, 2.1.0.0 ... the last number stays 0).
  2. Double-click "Build Store package.cmd".
  3. Partner Center > your app > Update > Packages: replace the .msix > Submit.
  Windows updates everyone's copy by itself.

NOTE: Microsoft signs the package with your publisher name, so people see you as the publisher
and get no "Unknown publisher" warning. The Setup.exe from Build.cmd still works, but it keeps
showing the warning; use the Store link to share the app.
